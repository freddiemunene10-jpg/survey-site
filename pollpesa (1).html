How PollPesa works

  1. Joining is free. There are no sign-up fees, ever.
  2. Answer surveys. Businesses pay to ask questions, and you earn a reward for each one you finish.
  3. Withdraw to M-Pesa. Cash out from KES 200 (KES 10 fee). Requests are reviewed before they are sent.

Prototype: balances are saved on this device only. Real payouts need a backend and funds from survey sponsors.
